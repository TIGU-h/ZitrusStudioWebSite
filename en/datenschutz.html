We store personal data only for as long as necessary for the respective purpose or as required by legal retention obligations. Inquiries are deleted once they are no longer needed, unless there are legal reasons for longer storage.

7. Your rights

Under applicable law, you have the right to access, rectification, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection to certain processing activities. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

We reserve the right to amend this privacy policy if technical, legal, or organizational circumstances change.
